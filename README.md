## Research themes

| | Theme | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| *ϑ \*RR* | **Chern–Simons modified gravity** | Parity-violating extensions of general relativity and what they predict for compact objects and cosmology. |
| *δg<sub>μν</sub>* | **Black hole perturbation theory** | Axial and polar perturbations of charged and rotating black holes in modified gravity backgrounds. |
| *⟨0\|T<sub>μν</sub>\|0⟩* | **QFT in curved spacetime** | Bound states and particle production in time-dependent backgrounds, including breather-type field configurations. |
| *h<sub>L</sub> ≠ h<sub>R</sub>* | **Gravitational-wave phenomenology** | Observable signatures of parity violation, such as birefringence in primordial and astrophysical gravitational waves. |

I work analytically wherever possible and use Python and Mathematica for the parts that need numerics and visualisation.
